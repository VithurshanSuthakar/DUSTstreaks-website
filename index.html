01 / Dataset

A public view of RSO streaks from the stratosphere.

DUSTstreaks combines calibrated image products, COCO-format bounding boxes, preserved vector endpoints, transit identifiers, derived morphology, image-level cross-validation splits, fold-level evaluation results, and 20 trained detector checkpoints.

The observations capture the geometric and signal diversity encountered by a balloon-borne star tracker, supporting reproducible development and comparison of single-image streak detectors.

02 / Processing

From calibration frames to detector-ready stacks.

Master bias and flat calibration are followed by percentile stretching, median denoising, and three-channel conversion. Plate-solving products were generated from the stacked FITS images before these display-oriented preprocessing steps.

03 / Characterisation

Measured morphology, without over-interpreting identity.

The released measurements describe the observed streak population. Cross-streak half-maximum width is derived from five background-corrected profiles; signal quality is defined operationally from ROI intensity dispersion. Six exploratory morphological groups summarize concentrations in the standardized feature space rather than physical or orbital classes.

04 / Detection benchmark

Four detectors, one common operating point.

All architectures were evaluated across the released five image-level folds at matching IoU 0.5. Fixed-threshold F1 at confidence 0.5 is the primary standardized comparison; retrospective best F1 and AP50 describe the broader precision–recall behaviour.

How to interpret the released folds

The partitions compare architectures consistently within DUSTstreaks, but they do not constitute transit-held-out evaluation: 94.7% of test-fold images belong to a transit also represented in the corresponding training fold. The results therefore should not be read as performance on previously unseen transits or flight campaigns.

05 / Qualitative results

Detector behaviour across representative morphologies.

Examples are selected from the transit nearest each cluster centroid. They illustrate model behaviour for representative observations and are not group-level detection rates.

06 / Access & citation

Designed for reproducible reuse.

The dataset, measurements, evaluation records, and weights are archived together on Zenodo. Analysis and evaluation code are maintained on GitHub. Both are released under CC BY-NC 4.0.
